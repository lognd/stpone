% Chapter 8: beeps and alarms.
\chapter{Beeps and alarms}\label{ch:beeps}
\index{beeps}\index{alarm}

The machine has one buzzer and only a few sounds. Learn them; they tell you
the result of a wind without looking.

\begin{center}
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.5}
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{0.30\textwidth}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{0.22\textwidth}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}X@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Sound} & \textbf{When} & \textbf{What to do} \\
\midrule
\textbf{Three short beeps}\par
  {\small (beep 0.3 s, gap 0.15 s, three times)} &
  Wind finished, \shows{DONE}. &
  The roll is at least full length plus margin. Cut and remove it. \\
\textbf{Three long beeps, repeated}\par
  {\small (beep 0.7 s, gap 0.25 s, three times; repeats every 6 s, up to 10
  times, about one minute)} &
  \shows{!! UNDER TARGET !!} with \shows{SHORT}. &
  The roll is short. Push any button to silence. Find the cause
  (Section~\ref{sec:under-target}). \\
\textbf{Silence} &
  You stopped a wind with \btn{1} or \btn{2}. &
  Read the screen: \shows{DONE} or \shows{STOPPED}. \\
\textbf{Buzzer stops suddenly} &
  A new wind was started. &
  Normal: starting a wind always silences the buzzer. \\
\midrule
\textbf{Three short beeps} on finish \emph{and} on stop &
  Only in legacy mode (bump setting 0, Section~\ref{sec:set-bump}). &
  No guarantee in this mode; check every roll. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{center}

\section*{Notes}
\begin{itemize}
  \item There is no beep at power-up and no beep when you push a button.
  \item The alarm stops by itself after about one minute, but the
        \shows{UNDER TARGET} screen stays until someone pushes a button. If
        you come back to a silent machine showing \shows{UNDER TARGET}, the
        last roll is short.
  \item A power cut or a controller reset silences everything and loses the
        result.
\end{itemize}
