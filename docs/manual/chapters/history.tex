\section*{Firmware}
\begin{center}
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.4}
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}l>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}X@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Version} & \textbf{What changed for the owner} \\
\midrule
fw=4 & Result screen shows the final length after the roll stops, with a
  speed figure for technicians. \\
fw=5 & Saving settings no longer pauses the machine; more diagnostics for the
  PC program. \\
fw=6 & Trim mode with the minimum-amount guarantee: safety margin, final
  bumps, \shows{DONE} / \shows{!! UNDER TARGET !!} screens and the alarm.
  New settings format (WIN6): settings from older firmware are not used;
  calibrate once after updating. Panel buttons: every push of a latching
  button is one click; a button pushed in at power-up is ignored.
  Later fw=6 builds: factory coast 2 yards and bumps sized from the glide
  measured in the same roll (shorter final bumps, trim up to 45 s); saved
  settings keep their old coast until \ui{Reset to factory settings} and
  Save. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{center}
