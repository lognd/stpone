\chapter{Known limits}\label{ch:limits}
\index{limits}

This machine is honest about what it cannot do. Please read these points.

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=0.6em]
  \item \textbf{No jam detection during the main run.} The machine only
        notices that the paper has stopped during the trim at the end. A jam,
        tear or empty supply in the middle of a wind is not detected; the
        motor keeps running. Stay with the machine while it winds.
  \item \textbf{The guarantee trusts the measuring wheel.} If the wheel slips,
        is dirty, worn or lifted, the count is wrong and \shows{DONE} can be
        wrong too. Keep the wheel clean and calibrated.
  \item \textbf{Overrun figures are design estimates.} The expected overrun of
        about 1.3 inches on average (worst about 14 inches) comes from the
        design calculations and has not yet been confirmed on the bench. If the
        coast turns out to be twice what was assumed, the estimates become about
        3.6 inches average and 26.5 inches worst case.
  \item \textbf{On/off motor only.} There is no speed control, so the motor
        always winds at full speed and coasts at the end.
  \item \textbf{Screen glitches.} Relay noise can garble the screen briefly. It
        repairs itself within about 2 seconds; the wind is not affected.
  \item \textbf{A reset loses the result.} After a power cut or watchdog reset
        the machine forgets the wind in progress and its result.
  \item \textbf{Two lengths on the panel.} Only the short (30 yd) and long
        (90 yd) targets are on buttons. Other lengths need the settings changed
        from the PC, or a manual run.
  \item \textbf{Margin and legacy mode are not in the PC program.} They can only
        be changed by a technician over the serial command line.
  \item \textbf{Chromebook use is untested.}
  \item \textbf{Bench tests not yet signed off.} The expected values in the bench
        self-test (Appendix~\ref{app:bench}) come from the design and source
        code, not yet from a measured machine.
\end{enumerate}
