Board & Pro Micro (ATmega32U4), firmware fw=\fwver \\

  ARM64); Chromebook via Linux (untested) \\
Web page & http://127.0.0.1:8765 on the PC itself \\
Test mode safety & motor stops if the PC is silent for 5 s \\
